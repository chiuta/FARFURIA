# FARFURIA

**English** · [Română](README.ro.md) · [Français](README.fr.md) · [Italiano](README.it.md) · [Español](README.es.md) · [Português](README.pt.md) · [Deutsch](README.de.md) · [Magyar](README.hu.md)

A free digital dietitian: personalized calorie, macro and meal-plan guidance based on recognized public-health sources. One HTML file, works offline.

**[Open the app →](https://chiuta.github.io/farfuria/)**

_Version: 1.0 · Last updated: 2026-11-06_

## Features

- Calorie and macro targets (Mifflin–St Jeor, activity level, goal)
- 7-day meal plan scaled to your target, with diet and allergen filtering
- Food database with nutrient lookup, food diary and weight tracking
- Safety rules: calorie floors, capped deficit, locks and referral advice for minors, eating-disorder history and kidney disease
- 8 languages, light/dark theme, phone/tablet/desktop layouts, print-friendly
- Private: everything stays in your browser (localStorage); no server, no tracking, no network requests

## Use

Open the live page, or download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. No installation, no build.

## Important notice

Educational tool — **not medical advice**. It does not diagnose or treat. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, a minor, have a medical condition or take medication.

## Method and limits

Energy needs use Mifflin–St Jeor × activity factor; protein per kg by goal and activity; meal plans are assembled from fixed templates over a database of 61 foods and scaled to the target. Values are estimates. The app is not a substitute for individual assessment.
